# How to read the diagrams

<img src="/views/legend.png" class="view-legend" />

<div class="grid2 notes">
<div>

- Stages run **left to right**; stores sit next to the stages that use them.
- Click a stage in the interactive diagram to open it.
- Loops back ("fix", "new work item") are shown on the stage views.

</div>
<div>

- Tools (GitHub, Jira, Dokploy) are only labels on stores; the design does not depend on them.
- Every part has a status: **built** · **planned** · **idea** (faded on the status view).

</div>
